# Hackathon Demo Flow: The "Golden Path"

This document outlines the recommended script and flow for presenting TaskProof to the judges.

## Scene 1: The Vision (Landing Page)
1. Start at `http://localhost:5173/`.
2. Introduce the problem: "AI companies and researchers need high-quality human feedback, but sourcing reliable micro-task workers is hard and lacks transparency."
3. Introduce TaskProof: "A verified marketplace connecting businesses with contributors, powered by cryptographic proof of work."
4. Scroll through the landing page highlighting the sleek design and value proposition.
5. Click **Try Demo**.

## Scene 2: The Contributor Experience
1. On the signup page, select **I'm a Contributor**.
2. **Dashboard**: Show the greeting, gamified metrics (Level, Streak, XP), and current TCR balance. Emphasize that contributors build a verified reputation.
3. **Marketplace**: Navigate to **Tasks** via the sidebar. Show how contributors can filter tasks by category (e.g., AI Evaluation).
4. **Task Details**: Click on a task. Explain the requirements and transparency of the reward system.
5. **Completion**: Click **Start Task**. Step through the interactive form fields, showing how dynamic inputs work. Click **Submit**.
6. Show the success screen explaining that the submission is now awaiting business review.

## Scene 3: The Business Experience
1. Log out (via sidebar) and return to the signup page.
2. Select **I'm a Business**.
3. **Dashboard**: Show the analytics and active metrics. Highlight that businesses only pay for approved work.
4. **Reviewing Work**: Go to **Submissions** (or click the recent submission).
5. Explain the **Automated Pre-checks** feature (AI-assisted verification to save time).
6. Review the submitted work and click **Approve & Reward**. Show the success banner confirming the release of TCR and generation of the blockchain proof hash.
7. **Task Creation**: Go to **Create Task** and quickly show the 5-step wizard and live budget calculator.

## Scene 4: Verification & Trust
1. Switch back to the Contributor (Log out -> I'm a Contributor).
2. Go to **Wallet**. Show the newly received transaction and point out the **Blockchain Verified** badge.
3. Go to **Profile** to show how the approved task has been permanently added to their verified work history.
4. Conclude the presentation summarizing the impact: "TaskProof ensures quality for businesses and fair, verified compensation for workers."
