# TaskProof Demo Walkthrough

This document outlines the ideal flow for demonstrating TaskProof's features during the DEV2HACK presentation.

## Preparation
Ensure both the FastAPI backend and Vite frontend are running:
1. Backend: `python -m uvicorn app.main:app --port 8000 --host 127.0.0.1` (in `backend/`)
2. Frontend: `npm run dev` (in root)

## Flow 1: The Business Experience (Task Creation & Review)

1. **Login**: Go to the landing page and click **"I'm a Business"**. You will automatically be authenticated as the demo business (TechCorp).
2. **Dashboard Overview**: Briefly show the Business Dashboard. Point out the active tasks, approval rates, and dynamic analytics chart (powered by backend aggregation).
3. **Task Creation**:
   - Click "Create New Task".
   - Proceed through the 5-step wizard.
   - Enter a title, choose a category (e.g., AI Evaluation), set a budget, and publish it.
   - Highlight the clean UI and dynamic budget calculator.
4. **Submission Review**:
   - Navigate to the "Review" tab in the sidebar.
   - Show how businesses can see submissions needing review.
   - Click on a pending submission, review the automated pre-check status, and click **Approve & Reward**.
   - Show the success banner indicating the TCR reward has been atomically processed and a transaction hash generated by the backend ledger.

## Flow 2: The Contributor Experience (Completion & Rewards)

1. **Switch Roles**: Log out from the sidebar. Click **"I'm a Contributor"** to authenticate as the demo contributor.
2. **Task Marketplace**:
   - Go to "Tasks". Show the marketplace with dynamic filtering and search.
   - Find the task created by the Business in Flow 1 (or any existing seeded task).
   - Click on it to see the requirements and click **Start Task**.
3. **Interactive Task Completion**:
   - Step through the task fields. Demonstrate how required fields block progress if empty.
   - Submit the task. Show the "Task Submitted" success screen.
4. **My Submissions**:
   - Navigate to "My Tasks" to track submission status.
   - Show how the previously approved task in Flow 1 now appears as "Approved" with the reward visible.
5. **Wallet & Provenance**:
   - Go to "Wallet".
   - Highlight the **Task Credits (TCR)** balance.
   - Show the detailed transaction history.
   - Click **View Proof** on a transaction to display the simulated blockchain proof hash.
6. **Profile Progression**:
   - Go to "Profile".
   - Demonstrate the gamification elements: Level, XP, Streak, Quality Score, and earned Badges.

## Key Talking Points for Judges
- **Full-Stack Architecture**: Highlight that this isn't just a mock UI; it's backed by a real SQLite database, FastAPI backend, and robust API design.
- **Atomic Transactions**: Mention the ledger system guarantees rewards are safely deducted and credited without race conditions.
- **Premium UX**: Emphasize the glassmorphism aesthetic, fluid Framer Motion animations, and zero-latency feel provided by optimistic UI updates and efficient backend loading.
